# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-focused research
community. Constructive disagreement about methods and results is welcome;
harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and deliberate misrepresentation
of evidence are not.

Contributors should distinguish observations from interpretations, acknowledge
uncertainty, respect data-use restrictions, and give appropriate credit.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the
community and research assets.

This concise policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, reviews,
and project events.
